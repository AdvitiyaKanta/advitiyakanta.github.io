About

Level Designer working in Unreal Engine 5. Based in Dundee, Scotland. From Shimla, India.

I got my professional start mid-degree: seven months at Kahr Works GmbH designing compounds for Revolver Rift, an AA PvPvE extraction shooter in development. 2D layouts, blockouts, combat flow, weekly level reviews, the real production loop.

Since then I have shipped two client projects. To Pierce Eternity for Hyperluminal, where I owned the player space on a team of twelve, and Hull & Horizon for the V&A Dundee, where I led level design and playtested on the museum floor.

What I care about is levels that read without words. Composition, landmarks and light do the guiding, and UI is the last resort. Every project here applies that under a different constraint: a competitive compound, a lying HUD, a museum deck, a one-room card den.
