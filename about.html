About

Level Design built on readable space and observable decisions.

I am a Level Designer based in Dundee, with seven months of commercial experience at Kahr Works and an MProf Games Development from Abertay University. I am open to relocation across the UK.

My strongest work starts before the engine: a brief, bubble diagram, beat map or 2D plan that makes route and pacing decisions visible. In UE5 I use blockout, sightlines, landmarks, leading lines and spatial contrast to test those decisions at player scale.

The portfolio is deliberately evidence-led. Revolver Rift is my professional credential; To Pierce Eternity shows the full plan-to-blockout-to-revision chain; and Hull & Horizon shows how ten museum-floor sessions changed a design.
