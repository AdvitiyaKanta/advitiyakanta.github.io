About

I'm a Level Designer focused on how players read, navigate and move through space.

Based in Dundee, with seven months of commercial Level Design experience at Kahr Works on Revolver Rift, and client-brief work for Hyper Luminal Games and V&A Dundee. Open to remote roles and relocation.

My work starts before the engine. A bubble diagram, a beat map or a 2D plan makes route and pacing decisions visible while they are still cheap to change. In Unreal I build those decisions as blockout and test them at player scale — sightlines, landmarks, leading lines, elevation, and the order in which things appear.

I care most about the moment a player decides where to go. If that decision is a guess, the layout is doing something wrong, and no marker or prompt fixes it properly. Most of my design time goes into making that read obvious without a prompt.

I have worked inside a studio pipeline under NDA, to two external client briefs, and solo. The part that carried across all three was documentation: intent written down clearly enough for other people to build against, and revised when the evidence said to.

How I work

Design
Start with player intent, route structure and spatial relationships.
Build
Translate plans into playable blockouts and greyboxes.
Test
Observe where players hesitate, misread or lose the intended flow.
Iterate
Use that evidence to change the level.
Collaborate
Discuss intent, take feedback, adapt to other people's work and integrate other disciplines into the playable space.
